Inkwild: Hunter's Journal

Track deer, moose, wolves and bears through quiet northern forests, take one clean shot, and keep every hunt in your own field journal of ink sketches and photos.

In the journal

  • Seven northern species to find and study: doe, red deer, reindeer, moose, wolf, fox and bear. Wolves and bears can charge.
  • 60 hunts across a summer forest, an autumn forest and the winter taiga, and a new Daily Hunt every day.
  • A naturalist's notebook that fills with a sketch of every animal and photos of your best shots.
  • Free to play with ads. No in-app purchases.

Coming soon to Google Play.

Privacy

The game has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. It uses analytics and ads; our privacy policy explains what is collected, why, and the choices you have.
