Privacy Policy

Inkwild: Hunter's Journal · effective 24 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Inkwild: Hunter's Journal (“the game”) collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. The game is made by Inkwild and published on Google Play by TBkid (“we”, “us”).

At a glance

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress, settings and journal photos stay on your device.
  • We use Google's Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played.
  • The game is free and shows ads. Ads come through CAS.AI, which works with several ad networks. They use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads.
  • Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings.
  • The game is meant for adults (18+). It is not directed at children.

1. Who we are

Inkwild is the name of the game and of its developer brand. The game is published on Google Play by TBkid, which is responsible for the personal data described here (the “controller”).

For anything about privacy, write to inkwild.developer@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

The game saves these on your phone or tablet only:

  • your progress, coins and weapons;
  • your settings: sound on or off, and aim sensitivity;
  • your journal: the animals you have found and the “photos” of your best shots and Daily Hunts, which the game draws from its own scene during play (they show the game world only).

We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include it in your own device backup in your Google account.

3. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics, a service from Google, to see how players use the game: which levels are too hard, where players stop, and whether something is broken. Firebase collects:

  • a random ID created for this installation of the game (the app-instance ID);
  • what happens in the game: the screens you open, levels started, won or lost (with the number of animals taken and why a hunt ended), when you continue a hunt, and when you double a reward by watching an ad; plus Firebase's standard events, such as the first opening of the game and the length of a session;
  • device and app details: device model, Android version, app version, language and region;
  • your approximate location (country, region or city), worked out from your IP address;

Firebase in this game does not read your advertising ID and is not used for advertising. Until the game knows whether your consent is needed where you are (it checks with the consent form after your first hunt), and wherever you have not agreed, Firebase keeps no ID on your device and sends only basic counts without the app-instance ID.

Firebase does not tell us who you are, and we do not link this data to your name or email. Google processes it on our behalf. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by ads. Ads are delivered through CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions), an ad mediation service that picks an ad network for each ad. When the game asks for an ad, CAS.AI and the chosen ad network may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, and Android's app set ID;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • device details: model, Android version, language, screen size, mobile carrier and connection type;
  • app details: the game's package name and version;
  • how you interact with ads: which ads were requested, shown, clicked or watched to the end;
  • diagnostics about the ad software, such as performance data and crash reports.

They use this to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results, pay us for the ads shown, and detect fraud. Where the law requires your consent, ads are personalised to your interests only if you agree.

The ad partners decide for themselves how they use this data, under their own privacy policies (they are independent controllers).

6. Your choices

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another place where the law requires it, the game shows a consent form after your first hunt, before any ad is loaded; the first hunt has no ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.

Advertising ID

In your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads) you can reset your advertising ID or delete it. After you delete it, apps can no longer read it. You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.

United States

We do not sell your data for money. However, under some US state laws (such as California's), the use of advertising data by ad partners can count as “selling” or “sharing” personal information. You can opt out through Settings → Privacy choices where the form offers it, by deleting your advertising ID as described above, or by writing to us.

Stop all collection

Uninstalling the game stops all data collection by the game.

7. Why we use data

  • To run the game, including giving you the rewards you choose to watch ads for.
  • To improve the game through analytics, and to find and fix problems.
  • To show ads that pay for the game, and to measure them.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the game and the ads secure.
  • To meet legal obligations.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, our legal bases are: your consent for personalised ads and wherever else the law requires consent; our legitimate interests in improving the game, showing non-personalised ads and preventing fraud; and legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 6); this does not affect what happened before.

8. Who receives data

  • Google, which runs Firebase Analytics for us.
  • CAS.AI and the ad networks listed in section 5.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.
  • A new owner of the game, if it is ever transferred; this policy would continue to apply.

We do not run our own servers, and we keep no database of players.

9. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Firebase Analytics: event data linked to the app-instance ID is deleted after at most 14 months. Summary reports with no identifiers may be kept longer.
  • Ad partners: as described in their own policies (section 5).
  • Emails you send us: only as long as needed to deal with your message.

10. Security

Firebase Analytics sends data over encrypted connections (TLS). Our ad partners describe their own security measures in their policies. The game asks for no special Android permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we choose established providers and collect as little as we can.

11. International transfers

Google, CAS.AI and the ad networks may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. They describe the safeguards they use, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, in their own policies.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict its use, receive a copy of it, and withdraw your consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To use these rights, email inkwild.developer@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. Because the game has no accounts, we usually can't tell which data is yours; uninstalling the game removes everything kept on your device. For advertising data, you can also contact the ad partners directly (section 5) or delete your advertising ID (section 6). We will help with either.

13. Children

The game is a hunting game meant for adults. It is not directed at children, its target audience on Google Play is 18 and over, and our ad requests are marked as not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data about them was collected, contact us and we will do what we can to have it deleted, including asking our partners.

14. This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security (see the GitHub General Privacy Statement). Its fonts are served from this site, so your browser connects to no one else.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell you in the game. Earlier versions remain visible in this website's public history.
